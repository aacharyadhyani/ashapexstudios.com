The Road Ahead

Coming Next

200–100 BCE

Iron of Kalinga

Primary Figure — Kharavela

Sunga Kalinga Indo-Greek

The age of Ashoka gave way to fragmentation. The mighty Kalinga, resurgent under warrior-king Kharavela, clashes against the Sungas and the lingering Indo-Greek kingdoms. A new power rises from the east.

Planned

100 BCE – 100 CE

Saka Wars

Primary Figure — Gautamiputra Satakarni

Satavahana Kshatrapas Sangam Kingdoms

The Satavahanas fight to reclaim the Deccan from Saka invaders. Gautamiputra Satakarni, the great king who shattered Saka power, leads the south's most dramatic resurgence while the Sangam kingdoms bloom along the coasts.

Planned

100–200 CE

The Silk Road

Primary Figure — Kanishka

Kushan Empire

Kanishka the Great stretches the Kushan Empire from Central Asia deep into the heart of India. Control the ancient trade routes of the Silk Road, spread Buddhism eastward, and forge one of history's most cosmopolitan empires.

Planned

300–400 CE

Allahabad Proclamation

Primary Figure — Samudragupta

Gupta Empire

The warrior-poet Samudragupta embarks on a grand campaign across India, carving his victories into the Allahabad pillar. No king dared stand before him — play his march and see if you can replicate his undefeated conquest.

Planned

400–500 CE

The Golden Age

Primary Figure — Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya)

Gupta Zenith Last Sakas

The Gupta Empire reaches its cultural and economic peak under Vikramaditya. Art, science, and literature flourish while the last Saka kingdoms are crushed. This is the India that the world would remember for a thousand years.
